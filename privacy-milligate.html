Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Milligate mobile app (available for iOS and Android) and explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. Milligate is operated by Team CodeLenz, a cooperation of self-employed individuals based in Austria.

1. Who is responsible

The party responsible for processing personal data in connection with Milligate is Team CodeLenz, reachable at office@team-codelenz.at.

2. What data we process

Depending on how you use Milligate, we process the following categories of data:

  • Account and profile data: your email address and password (stored encrypted) when you register and log in, used to manage your user account.
  • Tickets and orders: tickets you purchase, selected seats or parking spots, your order history, and the QR codes generated for entry.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe (see section 4). Milligate itself never stores full card details — these are handled exclusively by Stripe.
  • Location data: with your permission, the app uses your device location to show the distance to an event venue and directions on the map. This data is not stored permanently and is not shared with third parties.
  • Camera access: the app uses your device camera locally, only to scan QR-code tickets at entry. No photos or videos are taken or stored.
  • Push notifications: through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) we may send you optional notifications, e.g. about your events or tickets. This requires processing a device token.
  • Advertising and advertising identifier: Milligate shows ads via Google AdMob. This may use your device's advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads. On iOS we ask for your consent via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
  • Technical data: device and app information (e.g. app version, operating system) required for troubleshooting and the secure operation of the app.

3. Purpose of processing

We process this data to provide the app's core functionality: account management, ticket purchase and management, QR-code entry control, directions to the venue, keeping the app secure and functional, and — where you have consented — showing relevant advertising.

4. Sharing with third parties

To provide the app, we work with the following service providers, who process data as our processors or within their own area of responsibility:

  • Supabase: hosting of our database and user management (account, ticket, and event data).
  • Stripe: processing of payments (credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
  • Google (Firebase, AdMob): push notifications and the display of advertising.

We do not share your data with any third party beyond these service providers, unless required by law.

5. Data retention

We store your data for as long as your account exists, or as long as necessary for the purposes described above, in particular as proof of purchased tickets. Upon request, we delete your account and the associated data, unless legal retention obligations require otherwise.

6. Your rights

You have the right to request information about the data we hold about you, to request correction or deletion, to restrict processing, and to object to processing. You can also revoke location, camera, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Please contact us at the email address above to exercise these rights.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed, for example if Milligate's feature set changes. The current version is always available at this page.
